One system for every

BizCore ZA brings sales, stock, expenses, and employee data into one place — so South African SME owners can answer real business questions instead of guessing from notebooks and memory.

View on GitHub

Core Modules

Sales & Stock

Records every transaction against live stock levels, so inventory updates automatically and no sale goes unlogged.

"What should I stock more of, less of — and when?"

Expenses

Categorises every outgoing payment and maps it against income for a live profit/loss view the owner can actually act on.

"Where is my money going, and am I actually profitable?"

Employee Management

Links every action to the employee on shift — creating a clear, data-driven record of performance and accountability over time.

"Who was responsible, and who is actually performing well?"
